本隐私政策适用于 KetoPlan 生酮饮食计划（以下简称「本应用」），由 PureLifeLab 开发并维护。

This Privacy Policy applies to KetoPlan (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), developed and maintained by PureLifeLab.

1. 我们收集的信息

1. Information We Collect

本应用遵循「最小必要」原则，仅收集保障核心功能运行的必要信息：

The App follows the principle of "minimum necessary" and only collects essential information to ensure the operation of core functions:

  • 账户信息：注册时提供的邮箱、密码、昵称，用于账户创建与身份验证
  • Account Information: Email, password, and nickname provided during registration, used for account creation and authentication.
  • 身体数据：性别、年龄、身高、体重、目标体重、活动水平，用于计算每日宏量营养素目标和生成个性化饮食方案
  • Body Data: Gender, age, height, weight, target weight, and activity level, used to calculate daily macro nutrient targets and generate personalized meal plans.
  • 饮食偏好：饮食限制（如无乳制品、无坚果等）、过敏原、不喜欢的食物，用于过滤和定制食谱推荐
  • Dietary Preferences: Dietary restrictions (e.g., dairy-free, nut-free), allergens, and disliked foods, used to filter and customize recipe recommendations.
  • 设备基础信息：设备型号、iOS 系统版本、唯一设备标识符（IDFV），用于适配设备、修复崩溃、优化运行体验
  • Basic Device Information: Device model, iOS version, Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), used for device adaptation, crash fixing, and experience optimization.
  • 使用数据：功能使用频率、操作日志（匿名化处理），用于分析产品使用情况、优化功能体验
  • Usage Data: Function usage frequency, operation logs (anonymized), used to analyze product usage and optimize features.

2. 信息使用目的

2. Purposes of Information Use

  • 提供、维护和优化 KetoPlan 的核心生酮饮食规划功能
  • Provide, maintain and optimize the core ketogenic diet planning functions of KetoPlan.
  • 基于用户身体数据和饮食偏好，通过 AI 生成个性化膳食方案
  • Generate personalized meal plans through AI based on user body data and dietary preferences.
  • 计算每日宏量营养素目标（脂肪、蛋白质、碳水化合物、纤维）
  • Calculate daily macro nutrient targets (fat, protein, carbohydrates, fiber).
  • 排查应用崩溃问题，保障服务稳定运行
  • Troubleshoot app crashes and ensure stable service operation.
  • 管理订阅服务与高级功能访问权限
  • Manage subscription services and access to premium features.
  • 遵守相关法律法规要求
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. 数据存储与传输

3. Data Storage and Transmission

用户个人资料和饮食方案数据同时存储于本地设备和我们的云端服务器。云端数据通过加密传输（HTTPS/TLS），存储于安全的服务器环境中。

User profile and meal plan data are stored both on the local device and on our cloud servers. Cloud data is transmitted via encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in secure server environments.

AI 膳食方案生成服务由 Cloudflare Workers 提供。生成请求中仅包含必要的用户身体数据（性别、年龄、身高、体重、活动水平）和饮食偏好信息（饮食限制、过敏原、不喜欢的食物）。AI 推理可能通过以下服务提供商处理：

The AI meal plan generation service is powered by Cloudflare Workers. Generation requests contain only necessary user body data (gender, age, height, weight, activity level) and dietary preference information (dietary restrictions, allergens, disliked foods). AI inference may be processed through the following service providers:

  • Cloudflare Workers AI：部署于 Cloudflare 全球边缘网络，面向中国大陆以外地区用户提供 AI 模型推理
  • Cloudflare Workers AI: Deployed on Cloudflare's global edge network, providing AI model inference for users outside mainland China.
  • 阿里云百炼（Alibaba Cloud Model Studio）：面向中国大陆用户提供 AI 模型推理，数据处理位于中国大陆；国际版部署于欧盟法兰克福区域（eu-central-1），面向其他地区用户。应用根据用户 IP 所属地区自动选择对应的服务端点
  • Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Bailian): Provides AI model inference for users in mainland China (data processed in mainland China). The international version is deployed in the EU Frankfurt region (eu-central-1) for users in other regions. The App automatically selects the appropriate service endpoint based on the user's IP location.

AI 处理过程中，用户的身体数据和饮食偏好会以加密形式传输至上述服务商的 API 端点。上述服务商不会将用户数据用于模型训练或其他用途。

During AI processing, user body data and dietary preferences are transmitted in encrypted form to the API endpoints of the above service providers. These service providers do not use user data for model training or other purposes.

订阅交易通过 Apple StoreKit 2 处理，本应用不收集或存储任何支付卡信息。

Subscription transactions are processed through Apple StoreKit 2. The App does not collect or store any payment card information.

4. 信息安全

4. Information Security

我们采用行业标准安全措施保护用户数据，防止数据泄露、篡改和未经授权的访问。所有网络通信均通过 HTTPS 加密。我们不会向任何第三方共享、出售、出租用户的个人信息，法律法规要求除外。

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect user data against leakage, tampering, and unauthorized access. All network communications are encrypted via HTTPS. We will not share, sell, or rent users' personal information to any third parties except as required by laws and regulations.

5. System Permissions

The App does not request the following system permissions: Camera, Photo Library, Location, Health (HealthKit), Motion Sensors, Microphone. The App does not use any third-party analytics or advertising tracking SDKs.

6. AI 生成内容声明

6. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

本应用使用人工智能（AI）技术生成膳食方案和食谱建议。关于 AI 生成内容，请注意以下事项：

The App uses artificial intelligence (AI) technology to generate meal plans and recipe suggestions. Regarding AI-generated content, please note the following:

  • 仅供参考：AI 生成的所有膳食方案、食谱建议及营养信息仅供一般性参考，不构成医疗、营养或健康方面的专业建议
  • For Reference Only: All meal plans, recipe suggestions, and nutritional information generated by AI are for general reference only and do not constitute professional medical, nutritional, or health advice.
  • 可能存在不准确：AI 模型可能生成不完全准确、适用或最新的信息，特别是在涉及过敏原、食物相互作用、特殊健康状况等方面
  • Potential Inaccuracies: AI models may generate information that is not entirely accurate, applicable, or up-to-date, particularly regarding allergens, food interactions, and special health conditions.
  • 不替代专业建议：本应用不能替代注册营养师、医生或其他合格医疗专业人士的个性化建议。有特定健康状况、食物过敏或特殊饮食需求的用户，在采用 AI 生成的方案前应咨询专业人士
  • Not a Substitute for Professional Advice: The App is not a substitute for personalized advice from a registered dietitian, physician, or other qualified healthcare professional. Users with specific health conditions, food allergies, or special dietary needs should consult a professional before following AI-generated plans.
  • 数据处理范围：用于 AI 生成的用户数据仅限于身体数据和饮食偏好，不包含用户的姓名、邮箱等身份识别信息
  • Data Scope for AI: User data used for AI generation is limited to body data and dietary preferences. It does not include personally identifiable information such as name or email.
